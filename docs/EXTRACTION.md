# Extraction and simplification

Autonomio Resolvent 1.0 is an empty, general-purpose template derived from the
predecessor Resolvent design at revision `5963418ceff953e2a01b054df935416a0496b2a1`.
Only general research/ingestion process material is retained. No predecessor git
history, business data, claims, evidence or reports are imported.

## Retained and adapted

The activity contracts for research brief creation, research execution, research
handoff, interview, source material, simulation, intra-model reasoning, and liminal
research retain their epistemic safeguards. The staged liminal directive is retained
with its product-independent analysis stages. Normalization remains separate from
canonical admission. Research still yields brief, result, and final closure artifacts.

The dependency/coupling distinction and spec dimensions remain. Evidence/source
separation, explicit uncertainty, atomic claims, linkage and delta closure remain.
A new conversation input shim adds retrospective, no-new-research capture.

## Trimmed

Claims no longer require business owners, review cadence, specialized funding or
revenue metadata, or sub-world routing. Class, statement, uncertainty and evidence
are explicit. Retirement and questions are no longer mixed into the claim-class
list. Qualitative confidence is replaced by uncertainty with its direction reversed.

The company-specific SDK, financial simulation runtime, reporting system, large
compiler's domain rules, mutable YAML/TTL mirroring, local mutation receipts and
operator-router machinery are excluded. They would reinstate requirements deliberately
removed from this template. Small schema-driven validation/admission tools replace
only that incompatible runtime, not the epistemic research process.

## Minimal additions

One portable skill, one contribution envelope, UUID IDs for concurrent researchers,
conversation capture, admission/maintenance job contracts, a slim deterministic
compiler, an admission materializer, a bounded maintenance selector, reproducible
skill packaging, tests and GitHub workflows. No LLM provider integration or API key
is assumed. The organization's existing agents remain responsible for judgment.

Contracts distinguish executable schema invariants from semantic duties that require
agent review. A prompt cannot guarantee source verification or actual skill activation;
those are not falsely represented as deterministic checks.
